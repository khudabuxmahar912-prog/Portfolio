About me

I'm Khuda Bux Mahar, a Computer Systems Engineering student at Quaid-e-Awam University of Engineering, Science and Technology (QUEST), Nawabshah. I enjoy building software and have a particular interest in web development, where I like turning an idea into something people can open and use.

Beyond the web, I'm interested in AI and machine learning, AI automation, AI agents and API integration, and I'm learning how these fit together in real applications. Robotics is another area I follow because it connects software with the physical world.

Hackathons are where I practise most. Working under time pressure with a team pushes me to solve problems quickly and to learn whatever the project needs. I'm still early in my journey, so I treat every project as a chance to learn something new.
